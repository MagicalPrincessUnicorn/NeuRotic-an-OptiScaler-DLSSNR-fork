NEUROTIC

Use the complete current installer bundle and open NeuRotic.exe. Select or add a game in Installation Library, close the game, and review its installation plan. Use Uninstall for a managed installation.

Configure a compatible separately supplied neural-rendering model before enabling NR. NR Anything is available in the desktop App. For troubleshooting, export a diagnostic ZIP from the selected game's Diagnostics tab.

Keep the complete bundle together and preserve component licenses.
