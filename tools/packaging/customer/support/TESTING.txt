NEUROTIC — TROUBLESHOOTING

Record the game, GPU, driver, graphics API, rendering route and settings when reporting a problem. Include the diagnostic ZIP exported from Installation Library's Diagnostics tab. Preserve installation recovery records and personal files.

For image comparisons, keep game settings and scene conditions consistent. Check visible NR output, motion, menus, loading and transitions. Requested settings and running status do not by themselves establish successful processing. Enable file logging only when collecting a troubleshooting log.
